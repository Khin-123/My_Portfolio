# Portfolio visual direction

The user requested a general IoT and embedded-systems visual, a design informed by https://www.drone.io/, and light blue `#E9F1FA`, bright blue `#00ABE4`, and white `#FFFFFF`.

Drone's public stylesheet at https://www.drone.io/css/main-7e62b7d792.css declares Kanit. This portfolio uses locally served Kanit Medium for headings and system sans-serif for body copy. Fonts and the accompanying OFL license were downloaded from Google's official font repository at https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/kanit . Dark navy text and darker blue text supplement the supplied palette for readable contrast.

## Hero asset

File: `public/images/iot-ecosystem.png`

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. This is a conceptual illustration, not a photograph of the student's projects or a claim about implemented features.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: portfolio website hero illustration. Create a refined isometric editorial technology illustration of a general Internet of Things and embedded systems ecosystem, like crafted vector-style dimensional illustrations on developer platform websites. A small connected city of objects: generic unbranded circuit board with tiny chips, a smart home, environmental sensor, wireless antenna, cloud, and robotic actuator, connected by elegant thin cyan circuit paths over several floating layered platforms. Balanced cohesive scene, crisp detailed geometry, subtle soft shadows, white and light blue #E9F1FA materials with bright cyan #00ABE4 accents, limited deep navy structural lines. Pale blue #E9F1FA background. Landscape composition 3:2, centered scene fills canvas with modest breathing room, no people, no text, no letters, no logos, no ESP32 or specific board. Professional sophisticated illustration, not clipart, not a diagram of a single microcontroller.
